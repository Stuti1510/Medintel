## Diagnostic Criteria (DSM-5-TR)
Diagnosis requires >= 5 of 9 depressive symptoms present during the same 2-week period, representing a change from previous functioning; at least one symptom must be either depressed mood or loss of interest/pleasure (anhedonia).

- SIGECAPS criteria: Sleep disturbances (insomnia/hypersomnia), Interest loss (anhedonia), Guilt/worthlessness, Energy depletion/fatigue, Concentration deficits, Appetite/weight fluctuations, Psychomotor agitation/retardation, Suicidal ideation.

## Screening & Risk Stratification
- Standardized instruments: PHQ-9 (Patient Health Questionnaire-9), Beck Depression Inventory (BDI).
- Safety assessment: Active evaluation of suicidal ideation, intent, plan, and access to lethal means.
- Medical rule-outs: Thyroid disease (hypothyroidism), anemia, vitamin D/B12 deficiencies, substance abuse.

## Treatment Protocols
- First-line pharmacotherapy: SSRIs (escitalopram, sertraline) or SNRIs (venlafaxine, duloxetine).
- Evidence-based psychotherapy: Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), Interpersonal Psychotherapy (IPT).
- Combination approach: Combined psychotherapy and pharmacotherapy demonstrates superior response rates for moderate-to-severe depression.
