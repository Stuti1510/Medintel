# Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD)

## Overview & Staging
Chronic Kidney Disease is defined by abnormalities of kidney structure or function present for >3 months, staged by Estimated Glomerular Filtration Rate (G1–G5) and Albuminuria categories (A1–A3).

## Key Clinical Findings
- **Early Stages**: Frequently asymptomatic; detected via routine biochemical panels (elevated serum creatinine, reduced eGFR, proteinuria).
- **Advanced Uremic Manifestations**: Peripheral pitting edema, periorbital puffiness, refractory hypertension, fatigue, pruritus, metallic taste, nausea, nocturia, and frothy urine.

## Diagnostic Workup
1. **eGFR Calculation**: CKD-EPI equation using serum creatinine and cystatin C.
2. **Urinary Albumin-to-Creatinine Ratio (uACR)**: Quantifies glomerular barrier damage.
3. **Renal Ultrasonography**: Evaluates kidney size, parenchymal echogenicity, and excludes obstructive nephropathy.
4. **Metabolic Bone & Hematologic Panel**: Serum calcium, phosphate, intact PTH, vitamin D, and hemoglobin (normocytic anemia of CKD).

## Clinical Guidance
- Renoprotective therapy: SGLT2 inhibitors, ACE inhibitors or ARBs, and non-steroidal mineralocorticoid receptor antagonists (finerenone).
- Strict blood pressure optimization (systolic BP <120 mmHg standardized) and cardiovascular risk reduction.
- Nephrology referral for G4/G5 staging to plan vascular access and kidney replacement therapy.
