# MEDINTEL AI — Clinical Safety & Triage Protocols

## Safety Architecture
MEDINTEL AI is developed as an academic research prototype and clinical decision-support system. It explicitly prohibits autonomous diagnostic claims or pharmaceutical prescribing.

## Red-Flag Detection Matrix
1. **Acute Coronary Syndrome**: Triggered by substernal chest pressure with left arm/jaw radiation and diaphoresis. Flags high-priority emergency triage (immediate ECG and high-sensitivity troponins).
2. **Acute Stroke Signs**: Triggered by unilateral facial droop, arm weakness, or aphasia (FAST criteria).
3. **Severe Hypoxemia / Respiratory Distress**: Triggered by SpO2 < 90% or clinical dyspnea with gasping.
4. **Acute Surgical Abdomen**: Triggered by severe migrating right lower quadrant pain with rebound tenderness and guarding.
5. **Hypertensive Emergency**: Triggered by systolic BP ≥ 180 mmHg or diastolic BP ≥ 120 mmHg.

## Disclaimer Mandate
Every clinical analysis output generated by the API or displayed in the UI includes the formal decision-support disclaimer:
*"CLINICAL DECISION-SUPPORT NOTICE: MEDINTEL AI is an academic research prototype. This system does NOT provide a formal medical diagnosis or prescribe clinical treatments. All outputs must be evaluated by a licensed healthcare professional."*
